# Your first operation

[Documentation](../README.md) · [Full player guide](player-guide.md)

**Goal:** seal two valves in North Atlantic and bring your six operators home. This is a suggested opening, not a fixed route: cover, contacts, and combat outcomes vary.

## 1. Launch and prepare

Extract the downloaded repository and open **ABYSSLINE.html** in a desktop browser. At Command:

1. Open **Research** and start **Acoustic masking**. It costs 16 Signal and completes after two strategic cycles; its noise reduction is not immediate.
2. Return to **Command** and select **North Atlantic**. Containment asks you to seal two valves, without an escort or demolition timer.
3. Choose **Prepare deployment**. Check that all six specialists are available, then **Begin operation**.

You can leave the initial equipment unchanged. The flagged Indian Ocean region counters a threat within three cycles; revisit that choice after learning the first mission.

## 2. Learn one operator's turn

Select an operator in the bottom roster or press **1–6**. Each begins with **2 action points (AP)**.

| Action | AP afterward, starting from 2 |
|---|---:|
| Move to a blue tile | 1 |
| Move to a gold tile | 0 |
| Fire, Overwatch, Brace, Suppress, or throw a depth charge | 0 |
| Reload, Interact, Extract, or use another specialist ability | 1 |

**Move before firing.** Click a visible hostile to inspect the hit chance, then press **F** or click **Fire**. A high percentage can still miss. Keep cover between the operator and the enemy; tall structures block sight.

Switch to another operator after committing an attack. Use **End turn** once you have finished the squad's actions; this starts the hostile phase and then restores actions to surviving operators.

## 3. Approach without waking everything

This mission begins concealed. Enemies can detect the squad within three tiles and line of sight, including along a movement route. Attacking, interacting with an objective, or reaching noise 4 also reveals everyone.

Move the team together and avoid rushing one specialist far ahead. If you can prepare an opening, set **Overwatch (G)** on supporting operators before another operator fires: enemy groups scatter when activated, which can trigger reaction shots.

## 4. Seal both valves

The objective panel lists **Array A** and **Array B**.

- With any operator, move adjacent to a valve and choose **Interact (I)** for 1 AP.
- With Engineer **RELAY**, use **Remote link (V)** within four tiles and line of sight for 1 AP.

Each seal reduces noise and flood damage. Both arrays must show **SECURED** before you withdraw.

Keep **PATCH**, the Medic, near injured operators. Field patch heals a living ally within two tiles and has two uses; it cannot revive someone who has died.

## 5. Check the hazards before End turn

| Read this | Respond like this |
|---|---|
| Noise meter approaching 7 | Limit unnecessary fire. Standard shots add 3 noise; at 7, an extra hostile arrives during the hostile phase. |
| Pressure surge warning | Leave striped tiles before the surge. They cause damage every third hostile phase. |
| Turn counter approaching 17 | Start returning early enough to move and extract everyone. |
| A wounded operator | Keep support nearby; do not leave them alone on the return route. |

Depth charges deal guaranteed damage but add 5 noise and hurt nearby allies. They are optional; see [explosives](player-guide.md#concealment-noise-and-flooding) before using one.

## 6. Extract everyone separately

When both valves are secured, return to the **green landing zone**.

1. Select an operator on a green tile.
2. Use **Extract (X)**. Have at least 1 AP available; a gold move spends both actions and leaves no action to extract that turn.
3. Repeat for every operator. Check the **operators aboard** count before leaving anyone behind.

The normal mission window is 17 turns. Anyone still deployed when time expires, or when you abandon the operation, is permanently lost. A mission can succeed with only one operator returned; aim for all six.

## 7. Read the debrief and prepare again

A successful Containment operation earns **34 Material and 16 Signal**, reduces North Atlantic's breach by three, and adds six Stability before other regions escalate.

The operation also advances one strategic cycle. Acoustic masking has one cycle left, other regions worsen, and injuries and threat countdowns advance. Open **Personnel**, check your available squad, then choose the next region using breach urgency, the flagged threat, and rewards.

Before stopping, use **☰ → Export save file**. Continue with the [player guide](player-guide.md) for all mission types and progression options.
